PECO-Buddy Closed Pilot Alpha 0.1.0 — Portable Edition
=======================================================

HOW TO RUN (no installation, no server)
---------------------------------------
1. Unpack this folder anywhere on your computer.
2. Double-click PECO-Buddy.html. The application opens in your browser.
3. Click Settings (gear icon, bottom left) and enter your API key once.
   It is stored in YOUR browser only — nothing is sent anywhere except
   your own AI provider and the public NLM medical terminology service.
4. Work through Step 1 (Ideation) to Step 3 (Outputs).

Your session is saved automatically in your browser — you can close the
page and reopen PECO-Buddy.html later; your work resumes where you left off.

REQUIREMENTS
------------
- Chrome, Edge, or Firefox (Safari is not recommended for this edition).
- An ordinary internet connection while using the tool (fonts, interface
  libraries, your AI provider, and the terminology service load over
  the network).

GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Keep the folder anywhere; nothing is installed on your computer.
- To share your work: Step 3 has the export buttons (protocol JSON,
  transparency report). Sending someone the whole folder does NOT send
  your work — your work lives in your browser until you export it.
- Clearing your browser data clears your saved session and settings.
- If the page ever looks broken after an update: hard refresh
  (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R).

This is an internal closed-pilot build for the working group.
